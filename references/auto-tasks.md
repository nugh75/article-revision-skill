# `/r-auto` task profiles

Read only the profiles named in the command. All profiles preserve evidence,
citations, numbers, epistemic strength, examples, headings, section order, and
the immutable scope unless a stricter profile says otherwise.

## `chiarezza`

Clarify syntax, referents, information order, and unnecessary density. Preserve
canonical terminology and logical relations. Stop when ambiguity reflects two
plausible conceptual interpretations.

## `stile`

Apply `tone-of-voice` to already sound prose: register, rhythm, concision, and
measured emphasis. Do not repair conceptual or argumentative defects under a
style label; report them as stop conditions.

## `connettori`

Correct or remove connective language only when the intended relation is
already unambiguous. Do not invent causality, contrast, consequence, or sequence
to make a transition smoother.

## `terminologia`

Apply an established canonical label consistently. The manifest must name the
canonical term and its allowed variants. Stop if variants may identify distinct
constructs or if no authoritative label is available.

## `citazioni`

Repair citation keys, placement, and formatting after bibliographic
verification. Do not add a source merely because it appears plausible or alter
a claim to fit a citation.

## `argomentazione`

Strengthen explicit premises, topic sentences, local inference, and
paragraph-to-paragraph progression inside the fixed scope. Stop before adding a
new claim, deleting relevant material, moving paragraphs or sections, or
choosing between defensible theoretical positions.

## Combined tasks

Apply the most constrained profile first: `terminologia`, `citazioni`,
`chiarezza`, `argomentazione`, `connettori`, then `stile`. Tag every proposal
with its profile. Integrate a unit once; do not generate separate overlapping
rewrites for each selected task.
